# TLDC and the PhD pillars
- Explainability: evidence spans, page references and attribution targets.
- Verifiability: legal claims, provisions, citations and explicit verification status.
- Privacy preservation: separated originals/research copies and re-identification assessment.
- Adversarial resilience: later tests for OCR corruption, misleading references, malicious retrieved content and prompt injection.
- Controlled self-evolution: version corpus and knowledge updates independently from model releases; require quality, privacy, legal-source, fairness, robustness and regression checks before updates. New documents must not automatically trigger retraining.
