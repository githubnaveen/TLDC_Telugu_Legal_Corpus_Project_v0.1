# Collection protocol
- Identify official source and document category.
- Review portal terms, court directions, access restrictions and permitted research use.
- Obtain institutional ethics guidance and legal review where appropriate.
- Do not bypass logins, CAPTCHA, rate limits or technical controls.
- Public access does not imply republication rights.
- Record source URL, portal, access date, original filename, checksum, collector, access conditions and processing history.
- Preserve originals in restricted storage.
- Assign a non-identifying `case_group_id` for leakage-safe evaluation.
